# Business Questions

The final SQL layer should answer questions such as:

1. Which cities have the highest average temperature?
2. Which cities have the highest and lowest observed temperatures?
3. What is the latest weather observation for each city?
4. What are the top three hottest observations for each city?
5. Which observations are hotter than their city's average?
6. How many hot/cold/normal observations does each city have?
7. What percentage of observations are hot?
8. How has temperature changed from the previous observation?
9. What is the three-observation moving average?
10. Which cities have alerts?
11. Which cities have weather observations but no alerts?
12. Which cities have alerts but no weather observations?
13. What is the daily average temperature by city?
14. What is the monthly temperature trend?
15. Where are observation gaps greater than the expected interval?
16. How do cities compare on temperature and wind?
17. What is the distribution/percentile position of each temperature?
18. Which cities have more hot observations than cold observations?

These questions will drive the Tableau and Power BI dashboard requirements.
